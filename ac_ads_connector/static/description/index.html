Overview

Marketing teams live in Ads Manager while sales lives in Odoo, and nobody can answer the only question that matters: what did each campaign really cost per won deal? This connector mirrors the full campaign structure of Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads and Google Ads into Odoo, pulls daily spend and performance with Meta's 28-day attribution restatements handled correctly, and lands every lead-form submission in CRM within minutes, deduplicated and fully UTM-attributed. Manage the platforms from Odoo too: pause or activate campaigns, edit budgets under the platforms' own rate limits, author whole campaigns as drafts that always arrive paused, build hashed customer-list audiences, and feed won CRM deals back to Google as click conversions so bidding learns from real revenue.

Key Features

Connect Meta Marketing API and Google Ads API accounts with one-click Test Connection, using the official SDKs.
Mirror campaigns, ad sets or ad groups, ads and creatives hourly with idempotent, conflict-aware sync.
Pull daily insight rows (spend, impressions, clicks, conversions) with 28-day restatement handling and monthly compaction.
Land lead-form submissions in CRM within minutes via polling plus optional real-time webhooks, with field mapping.
Detect duplicate leads by normalized email or phone with three configurable policies, never losing a submission.
Attribute every lead through UTM campaign, source and medium so standard CRM reporting works out of the box.
Pause, activate and edit budgets from Odoo with the platforms' rate limits respected automatically.
Author campaigns, ad sets and ads as drafts and push them live, always created paused for safety.
Upload hashed customer-list audiences (SHA-256 at push time, hashes never stored) to both platforms.
Send won CRM deals back to Google as click conversions, by gclid or Enhanced Conversions.
Follow every sync and push in dedicated run logs with per-record failure isolation and retry.
Compare spend against CRM leads and revenue in the built-in ROAS / Cost per Lead report.
Capture Google and Facebook click IDs (gclid/fbclid) on website leads - a capability no stock Odoo module offers.
Auto-tag ad landing URLs with UTM parameters, or use click-counting tracked short links.
Real confirmed-order revenue and ROAS per campaign with the Extend companion module.
Ad leads enter Marketing Automation nurture flows within minutes with the Extend companion module.
Built-in native spreadsheet dashboard for spend, clicks, leads and conversions.
Organic reach next to paid performance (Social app) with the Extend companion module.
